## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper or merchant, **I want to** sign in passwordlessly on an isolated multi-tenant store core, **so that** every store keeps its data private.

<objective>
Build the complete multi-tenant domain schema (`Product`, `Variant`, `SellingPlan`, `Order`, `OrderItem`, `OrderEvent`, `Subscription`) with mandatory `store` ForeignKeys, `StoreScopedManager`, and integer paisa `*_minor` fields per D-03 and D-04, wire `StorePublicMixin`, `StoreMerchantMixin`, and per-store `Customer` auto-provisioning endpoints per D-08, ship the idempotent `manage.py seed_brewmellow` command per D-05, and prove cross-tenant + cross-shopper HTTP `404` isolation in `backend/stores/tests/test_isolation.py` per FOUND-03, FOUND-04, FOUND-05, and AUTH-03.

Purpose: Lock down tenant data boundaries and flagship BrewMellow Tenant #1 parity on the backend in parallel with Wave 3 frontend work (`backend/**` files only).
Output: Multi-tenant `catalog`, `orders`, and `subscriptions` `0001_initial` migrations, `backend/stores/api.py`, `seed_brewmellow.py`, and `test_isolation.py`.
</objective>
